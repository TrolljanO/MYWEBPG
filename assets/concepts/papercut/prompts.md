# Prompts exatos — estudos papercut

As referências são imagens estáticas geradas com IA. As contagens de camadas nos prompts são intenções, não geometria comprovada no resultado.

## 1. Estudo A

Create one original art direction study, landscape aspect ratio 6:5, no text, no labels, no border, no UI. A single Japanese ukiyo-e ocean wave curls from the left toward the right around a spacious central opening. Frontal camera, whole wave visible, uncluttered white background. Strict black ink and white paper palette; gray ONLY from soft contact shadows. Fine engraved Hokusai-inspired hatching and elegant long curved lines. The INTERNAL CONTOURS OF THE WAVE are actual edges of separate thin cut-paper sheets stacked in depth. Gaps between these curved strips expose additional engraved curves on LOWER sheets, rather than blank holes. Each sheet stays flat in its plane. Visually plausible coherent closed silhouettes, deliberate negative-space apertures, crisp scissor-cut edges. Thin paper, NOT cardboard, not wood, no folds, no origami, no sculpted thick foam, no realistic ocean water, no blue, no mountain, no sun, no boat, no trees. Depth must occur INSIDE the wave, not simply foreground/background scenery. Maintain readable broad rhythms, avoid fractal lace and repetitive concentric tunnel geometry. Product-quality lighting from upper left, subtle shadows localized to overlaps, mostly frontal composition so this could become an interactive website illustration.
VARIANT A: FINE CUT. Use five primary thin sheets, shallow separation, broad breathing room between fine ink contours, clear crisp borders and only modest relief. At the wave crest show three especially legible overlapping cut tips, each exposing a drawn line on the next sheet underneath. The result should feel like a refined graphic woodblock print that has become real delicate paper layers, not a bulky sculpture.
